Good to know

  • FinTaxica has no accounts or servers of its own. All your data lives in your Google Drive, so you can delete it yourself at any time. You don't need to wait for us.
  • Want to keep a copy first? Open your sheet in Google Sheets and use File → Download.

Delete in the app

  1. Open FinTaxica and go to the More tab.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm. Your “FinTaxica Data” sheet and all its backup copies move to your Google Drive trash, and you are signed out.

Google permanently deletes trashed files after about 30 days. To delete them immediately, empty the trash in Google Drive.

Delete without the app

  1. Open drive.google.com or the Google Drive app, signed in with the account you used for FinTaxica.
  2. Search for FinTaxica Data.
  3. Delete the “FinTaxica Data” spreadsheet and any “FinTaxica Data — backup …” copies.
  4. Optionally, empty your Drive trash to remove them permanently right away.

Remove FinTaxica's access

Go to myaccount.google.com/connections, select FinTaxica and remove its access. Uninstalling the app from your phone also clears everything it stored on the device: your sign-in session, the link to your sheet and your preferences.
