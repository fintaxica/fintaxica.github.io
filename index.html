Fintaxica

Personal finance tracking where YOU own the data.

What it does

Track your cash, bank accounts and wallets in one place. Record income, expenses and transfers in seconds, see your balances and monthly spending at a glance — and over time, understand and optimise your taxes.

Your data stays yours

Fintaxica stores everything in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. We operate no servers and can never see your data. The app only receives permission to access files it creates itself — nothing else in your Drive. You can open, export or delete your data sheet at any time; it is a normal spreadsheet that belongs to you.
