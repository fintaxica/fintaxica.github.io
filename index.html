How it works

Set up in under two minutes.

Sign in with Google

FinTaxica asks for one narrow permission: access to the files it creates — nothing else in your Drive.

Your sheet is created

A spreadsheet named “FinTaxica Data” appears in your Google Drive. It belongs to you.

Add your sources

Enter today's balance for each bank account, wallet and cash. From then on, every entry keeps them up to date.

Private by design

We built FinTaxica so we can't see your money.

There is no FinTaxica server holding your data. The app talks directly to Google on your behalf, and your records live in a spreadsheet you own.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can FinTaxica see my financial data?

No. Your data is written to a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive and is never sent to a FinTaxica server — we don't operate one. The app can access only the files it created itself.

Does it read my SMS or connect to my bank?

No. FinTaxica never asks for SMS access or bank credentials. You record entries yourself, which keeps you in full control.

What happens if I change phones?

Install FinTaxica on the new phone and sign in with the same Google account. The app finds your existing sheet and everything is there.

Can I edit my data in Google Sheets directly?

Yes — it's a regular spreadsheet you can open, view and export at any time. If you edit it by hand, keep the column layout as it is so the app can keep reading it.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to More → Delete my data. Full steps, including how to do it without the app, are on the data deletion page.

How much does it cost?

FinTaxica is completely free right now, with every feature included. As it grows, we may introduce light, non-intrusive ads and a low-cost ad-free plan. Whatever we add, your financial data stays in your Drive and is never sold or used to target ads.
