Fintaxica — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

The short version

Fintaxica stores all of your financial data in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. We operate no servers, no databases, and no analytics backends. Your data never passes through us and we are technically unable to read it.

What access the app requests

When you sign in with Google, Fintaxica requests a single permission, Google's drive.file scope: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app." This means the app can only access files it created itself — your other Drive files, email, contacts and photos are not accessible to it.

What data the app stores, and where

  • The accounts you add (names, types, balances), and the income, expense and transfer entries you record, are written to a spreadsheet named "Fintaxica Data" in your Google Drive. It belongs to your Google account, not to us.
  • On your device, the app keeps only minimal technical state (your sign-in session and the ID of your spreadsheet) so it can reconnect.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or financial data. There are no ads, no trackers and no third-party analytics in the app.

Data & account deletion

You are in full control at all times:

  • Delete your data: delete the "Fintaxica Data" spreadsheet from your Google Drive. That removes all data the app ever stored.
  • Disconnect the app: sign out inside the app, and/or revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Uninstall: removing the app clears everything stored on your device.

Because we hold no copies of your data, there is nothing further for us to delete on our side.

Children

Fintaxica is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them (it collects no data from anyone).

Changes to this policy
