The short version

  • Your financial records are stored in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive — not on our servers. We don't operate any.
  • FinTaxica can access only the files it creates. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • We collect no personal or financial data. No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • You can delete everything at any time, from the app or directly in Google Drive.

1. Who we are

FinTaxica is a personal-finance app published by 100 Sangrama (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the FinTaxica app and this website. If you have any questions, contact us at fintaxica@gmail.com.

2. How FinTaxica works

FinTaxica has no backend of its own. When you sign in, the app creates a spreadsheet called “FinTaxica Data” in your Google Drive and reads from and writes to it directly through Google's APIs. Your records travel only between your phone and Google — they never pass through a server run by us, and we have no way to read them.

3. Google account access

FinTaxica uses Google Sign-In and requests a single permission, Google's drive.file scope:

“See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app.”

This lets the app create and manage its own spreadsheet (and backup copies of it). It does not give the app access to your other Drive files, Gmail, contacts, photos or any other Google data.

When you sign in, Google also tells the app which account you chose (your name and email address). The app relies on this only to keep you signed in on your device. It does not store it in your sheet or send it anywhere else.

5. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal or financial information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Google API Services — Limited Use

FinTaxica's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data accessed through Google APIs is:

  • used only to provide the features you see in the app;
  • never transferred to anyone else, except as required by law;
  • never used for advertising, and never sold;
  • never read by people, unless you ask us for help and share it with us yourself.

7. Sharing & third parties

We don't share your data, because we never have it. The only third party involved is Google, which provides sign-in and stores your spreadsheet as part of your Google account under the Google Privacy Policy. If you share your sheet with someone in Google Drive, that is between you and them.

8. Security

All communication between the app and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Your sheet is protected by your Google account's security, including any two-step verification you have set up. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is no FinTaxica database that could be breached. Keep your phone and Google account secure, as anyone with access to them can see your sheet.

9. Retention & deletion

Your data stays in your Google Drive for as long as you keep it there. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: More → Delete my data moves your sheet and all its backup copies to your Google Drive trash and signs you out. Google permanently deletes trashed files after about 30 days, or immediately if you empty the trash.
  • Without the app: delete the “FinTaxica Data” file and any “FinTaxica Data — backup” files from your Google Drive.
  • Revoke access: remove FinTaxica at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on your phone.

Full instructions are on the data deletion page. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

10. Your choices & rights

Because your data is in your own Google account, you can view, correct, export or delete it whenever you like, using the app or Google Sheets. Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct and erase personal data and to withdraw consent. Revoking FinTaxica's access withdraws your consent, and deleting your sheet erases your data. We're happy to help — email us.

11. Children

FinTaxica is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly process data from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the app after that date means you accept the updated policy.
